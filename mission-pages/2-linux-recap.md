### Hardening SSH
It is incredibly important that you ensure that you're ssh connections are kept safe, by hardening your ssh settings you make it harder for someone to gain access to your system. As someone with ssh access (especially if they have root access) is someone who has access to your entire system and potentially your entire network.<br>
<br>
When we say "Harden SSH" what it really means is removing password login and replacing it with SSH keys, which are essentially long random unique keys generated by your computer that are sent to the server to verify that you are who you say you are.<br>
Whilst there are other things to it, this is the main one.
